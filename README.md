Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyzes the Sample Superstore dataset using Python and Pandas to understand sales performance, profitability, regional performance, category performance, and yearly trends.

The analysis was completed in Jupyter Notebook and focuses on identifying patterns that can support better business decisions.

Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Jupyter Notebook
- Matplotlib

Analysis Areas

The analysis covers:

- Data overview and cleaning
- Overall business performance
- Profit by category
- Profit margin by category
- Profit by sub-category
- Regional performance
- Yearly performance
- Sales trends
- Profit trends
- Key findings and recommendations

Key Business Results

Metric| Result

Key Findings

Category Performance

- Technology recorded the highest total profit among the three categories.
- Technology also had the highest profit margin.
- Furniture had the lowest profit margin at approximately 2.49%.

Sub-Category Performance

- Copiers were the most profitable sub-category.
- Tables were the least profitable sub-category and generated an overall loss.

Regional Performance

- The West region recorded the highest sales and highest profit.

Yearly Performance

- Sales increased overall from 2014 to 2017.
- 2017 recorded the highest annual sales.
- Profit increased each year from 2014 through 2017.

Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Investigate the factors contributing to the low profitability of Furniture.
2. Review pricing, costs, and discount levels for Tables.
3. Identify the factors driving the strong performance of Technology and Copiers.
4. Investigate differences between regional sales and profitability.
5. Review discount patterns to determine their effect on profit margins.

Conclusion

The analysis shows that the Superstore business generated strong overall sales and positive profitability, but performance varied considerably across categories, sub-categories, and regions.

Technology and Copiers were particularly strong performers, while Furniture and Tables require closer attention to improve profitability.

This project demonstrates practical skills in data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, aggregation, profitability analysis, trend analysis, and communicating business insights using Python.
